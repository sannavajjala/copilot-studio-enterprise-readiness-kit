# Scoring Methodology

The score is a community decision-support model, not a Microsoft certification, security guarantee, or compliance attestation.

## Principles
1. **Evidence beats intent.** Award points for implemented controls with evidence.
2. **Risk beats arithmetic.** Critical failures override a high score.
3. **Context matters.** Risk determines depth of review.
4. **N/A requires rationale.** Document why a control does not apply.
5. **Reassess material changes.** Authentication, sharing, knowledge, tools, channels, ownership, environments, and deployment changes can alter risk.

## Evidence
Examples include architecture diagrams, policy configuration, environment records, deployment history, access tests, test results, monitoring views, approvals, and owner acknowledgements.

## Decision bands
- **90–100 — READY**
- **75–89 — READY WITH CONDITIONS**
- **0–74 — NOT READY**

Critical controls intentionally sit outside the arithmetic. Adapt them to organizational policy.
